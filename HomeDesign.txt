# AgentBlazer Home Page & Global Components Redesign Plan (UI/UX Pro Max)

## 1. Strict Constraints & Scope
- **ZERO MODIFICATIONS TO HERO VISUAL**:
  - `ParticleLogo.tsx`, `.logo-container`, `.logo-glow-*`, `OrbitingSocials.tsx`, or their positioning/props within `.hero-visual` are completely off-limits and must remain untouched.
- **ZERO CONTENT / API BREAKAGES**:
  - All copy, `siteConfig` values, routes, stats, social links, and component signatures remain strictly preserved.
- **THEME HARMONY & STRICT THEME-CORRECTNESS**:
  - All styles must use existing CSS variables (`--accent-primary`, `--accent-secondary`, `--surface`, `--surface-elevated`, `--border`, `--glow`, etc.) without hardcoding theme-specific colors.
  - Full dedicated support across Violet (dark cyber/purple), Inferno (dark magma/orange), and Frost (crisp light ice/#f0f6fa).

## 3. Detailed Component & Styling Specifications

### A. Hero Left Content (`Home.tsx` & `globals.css`)
1. **Club Badge**:
   - Elevated glass capsule with subtle backdrop-blur, thin theme border (`var(--border)`), uppercase typography with `letter-spacing: 0.08em`, and a subtle live pulse indicator in `var(--accent-primary)`.
2. **Hero Headline (`.hero-title`)**:
   - Typographic scale: `clamp(2.5rem, 5vw, 3.75rem)` with leading `1.15` and letter-spacing `-0.025em`.
   - Primary heading in `var(--text-primary)`, accented italic text styled with `var(--accent-primary)` and subtle text-glow in dark themes.
3. **Department / College Tag (`.hero-dept`)**:
   - Modern framed layout with an accent bar or pill container, setting clear institutional context without cluttering the headline.
4. **Hero Description (`.hero-description`)**:
   - Max measure: `540px`, line-height `1.75`, font size `1.05rem`, styled in `var(--text-secondary)` for optimal contrast.
5. **Action Buttons (`.hero-buttons`)**:
   - **Primary Button (`.btn-primary`)**: Theme gradient `var(--button-background)`, soft lift on hover (`translateY(-2px)`), crisp shadow `0 8px 24px var(--glow)`.
   - **Secondary Button (`.btn-secondary`)**: Frosted glass surface, `border: 1px solid var(--border)`, smooth hover transitions adapting per theme.

### B. Redesigned Stat Panel (`StatPanel.tsx` & `globals.css`)
- **Bento Metric Panel Layout**:
  - Encapsulated in an elevated glass container (`var(--surface-elevated)`).
  - **Differentiated Stat Hierarchy**:
    - Numerical counters (`8+`, `500+`) rendered in bold display typography (`2.25rem`, weight 800) with `var(--accent-primary)` gradient/glow.
    - Partnership item (`Salesforce` / `Active Trailblazer Mentorship`) styled with a distinct badge/tag visual distinction.
  - **Vertical Micro-Separators**:
    - Subtle 1px dividers (`var(--border)`) separating stat blocks on desktop, transforming into a responsive layout on mobile.
  - **Micro-Interactions**:
    - Subtle ambient hover glow and card border brighten.

### C. Polished Footer (`Footer.tsx` & `globals.css`)
- **Section Separation**:
  - Gradient border top using `var(--border)` and `var(--glow)` for a seamless transition from main content.
- **Brand Column**:
  - Refined logo framing with subtle glow, bold club typography, department & college affiliation details.
- **Quick Links & Contact Columns**:
  - Clear section headers in `var(--accent-primary)`, interactive link underlines/shifts on hover, structured contact information.
- **Bottom Sub-Footer**:
  - Clean divider, legible secondary text, proper copyright and association credits.

## 4. Dedicated Multi-Theme Treatment Strategy

### Dark Themes (Violet & Inferno)
- **Violet**: Background `#0a0a1a`, accents `#00d4ff` (cyan) & `#8b5cf6` (purple), glow `#00d4ff` / `#8b5cf6`.
- **Inferno**: Background `#1a0a0a`, accents `#ff6b35` (orange) & `#ff3d00` (red), glow `#ff6b35`.
- **Glassmorphism**: Translucent dark surfaces (`rgba(20, 20, 40, 0.7)` / `rgba(40, 20, 20, 0.7)`), rim lighting via borders, radial glows.

### Light Theme (Frost) - Explicit Custom Pass
- **Frost**: Background `#f0f6fa`, deep text `#0a2540`, slate secondary `#3a6085`, accents `#07b6d6` / `#4aa3df`.
- **Frost Glass Surface Treatment**:
  - High-opacity white/ice glass base (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.88)` to `rgba(240, 248, 255, 0.95)`).
  - Crisp cyan-tinted borders (`rgba(7, 182, 214, 0.35 - 0.45)`).
  - Soft diffuse ambient shadow (`0 8px 30px rgba(7, 182, 214, 0.12), 0 2px 8px rgba(10, 37, 64, 0.06)`) instead of dark outer glows, preventing washed-out or muddy cards.
- **Frost Contrast Protection**:
  - All text, secondary button labels, badges, and stat captions meet or exceed WCAG AA (>= 4.5:1) against `#f0f6fa` and frosted surfaces.
